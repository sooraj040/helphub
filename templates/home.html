{# Section 3: how HelpHub works, in four numbered steps. #}

How HelpHub works

You stay in control of the price and who you hire.

  1. Post your request

    Describe the job, where you are, your budget and when you need it done.

  2. Receive quotations

    Local workers send you their price and a message explaining how they would help.

  3. Choose your worker

    Compare the offers and select the worker that suits you best.

  4. Get it done and review

    Track the job until it's complete, then rate the worker to help others choose.
