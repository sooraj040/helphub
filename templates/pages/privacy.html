Privacy Policy

This is a draft and will be finalised before HelpHub launches.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address and password (stored securely as a hash).
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as your phone number and location.
  • The service requests, quotations and reviews you create on HelpHub.

How we use it

We use this information to run HelpHub: to let customers and workers find each other, to show quotations and job progress to the people involved, and to keep the platform safe.
