About HelpHub

HelpHub is a local service marketplace. Customers post the jobs they need done, from plumbing and electrical work to cleaning, painting and moving. Local workers respond with quotations, and the customer chooses who to hire.

We use quotations rather than automatic assignment so that customers stay in control of the price and the person doing the work. Workers get a simple way to find nearby jobs and build a reputation through reviews from the customers they help.
